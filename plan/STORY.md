## 0. Logline
Ella, a big-hearted, unflappable woman with Army-grade grit and blond curls that refuse to stay tied back, buys a giant wooden ark that a local eccentric left in the El Paso desert, for **one dollar**, and turns it into an animal shelter. Then a freak flood brings every weird animal in the Southwest to her gangplank two by two. A glowing watering hole starts making cartoon "love-is-in-the-air" mashups (a 30-foot wiener dog-python, a Chihuahua T-rex), and the only people rich enough to adopt them are the most ridiculous billionaires and celebrities on Earth.

**Tagline options:** "All creatures welcome. *Seriously.* All of them." / "Adapt. Overcome. Adopt." / "Two by two. Then it got weird."

## 1. Tone
- **South Park-style comedy:** a deadpan narrator, adults panicking about obvious things, town-hall meetings that go off the rails, local TV news that overreacts, and satire of billionaires, influencers, bureaucracy and "wellness." The absurd stuff gets treated as normal, and the normal stuff gets treated as absurd.
- **Ella is the hero, and she's the most competent person in every scene.** She's the calm straight-woman in a world losing its mind. Army references stay **light**: attitude and swagger only ("Roger that," "Adapt. Overcome. Adopt.," morning PT with the elephants, a salute to the Star on the Mountain at sunset). **Never Fort Bliss, and never any real rank, job or personal detail.** The jokes are never at the Army's or Ella's expense. She gets the last word, the coolest moments and the best one-liners.
- **Adult audience (players are 19-20+), South Park level:** raunchy and envelope-pushing, with crude jokes, innuendo, profanity and gross-out gags (a rhino fart is officially classified as a "haboob," poop gets its own ZIP code, Romance Hour gets a LOT of double entendres). **Guardrails:** the mashup mechanic stays cartoonish and **off-screen** (a sunset slow jam, a heart-shaped glitter cloud and a grumpy gig-economy stork with a crate the next morning), with **no explicit sex or nudity, nothing hateful, no real people, brands or politics**, and no animal is ever harmed. **Ella stays the hero**: the crude stuff happens around her, and she's the one with the best comebacks.
- **Settings toggle:** Humor = **Raunchy** (default) or **Cleaner**. Cleaner swaps every crude line for a tamer one (no profanity, toned-down innuendo, fart jokes become "toots") without changing the story or the gameplay.
- **El Paso love letter:** Franklin Mountains, the Star on the Mountain, Scenic Drive sunsets, Transmountain Road, monsoon season, spring dust storms, tumbleweeds, roadrunners, javelinas, the fall tarantula trek, green chile roasting season, the old San Jacinto Plaza alligators, "El Chuco," 915. The jokes are affectionate, never at locals' or Juárez's expense, and there are **no border or politics jokes.**
- **Local accuracy gag:** there are no saguaro cactuses (they don't grow in El Paso, and locals will notice). The plants are prickly pear, cholla, ocotillo, yucca, lechuguilla and creosote. A tourist character keeps pointing at things and calling them "a saguaro," and the narrator corrects him every time.

## 3. Structure at a glance
Four acts plus a finale, **20 chapters**. Each chapter is 3 to 5 in-game "Shelter Days" (one Day is about 4 minutes of play). That's roughly 8 to 10 hours for the full story, plus an endless **Sanctuary Mode** afterward.

| Act | Chapters | Title | What's new in play | Money scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 to 5 | **Grand Opening** | Dogs and cats, fast care decisions, first ads, first adoptions | $50 to $150 adoption fees |
| 2 | 6 to 10 | **Two by Two** | Exotic and impossible animals, habitats, special care, niche adopters | $1K to $25K donations |
| 3 | 11 to 15 | **Love Is in the Air (and the Water)** | The Puddle, Romance Hour, mashups, Mash-o-Scope matchmaking | $10K to $500K |
| 4 | 16 to 19 | **Lifestyles of the Rich & Furry** | Celebrity/billionaire adopters, screening interviews, red flags, bidding wars | $250K to $10M |
| Finale | 20 | **The Re-Floodening** | Set-piece rescue using mashup powers, the gala, the ending | Gala jackpot |

## 4. Act 1: Grand Opening (Chapters 1 to 5)
**Goal:** teach care, advertising and adoption fast, with lots of quick, funny decisions. Dogs and cats only.

1. **"A Dollar and a Dream"**: El Paso County auctions off a 300-foot wooden ark that has sat off the Loop 375 frontage road for 31 years. Ella is the only bidder (the other "bidder" was a tumbleweed that blew into the auction room). She buys it for $1. Under the hull she finds Sarge, a retired military working dog standing guard at his self-assigned post, who has clearly been there for years and has opinions. *Tutorial: feed, wash, pet.*
2. **"Cats, Dogs & Form 27-B"**: Inspector Plinth issues a provisional permit that he plainly hopes she'll fail. The first intake truck arrives: Chancla the chihuahua, Kevin the orange cat (one brain cell, shared with no one), Lowrider the corgi. *Teaches: kennel cleaning, Quick Calls (swipe decisions), first adoption.*
3. **"Going Viral (Slightly)"**: Dakota shows up for "volunteer hours" and starts the Adopt-a-Gram. Chancla's first post gets 11 likes. Dakota: *"We're basically famous."* *Teaches: the Ad Builder (photo + tags + medium) and adopter matching.*
4. **"Doc Lupe Makes House Calls"**: Churro the puppy ate Ella's hair ties (all of them; her curls are now free and wild). Doc Lupe arrives, unimpressed. A spring **dust storm** (haboob) turns every animal the color of a paper bag. *Teaches: vet checks and the Dust Storm wash-everyone rush.*
5. **"Inspection Day"**: Plinth's first inspection is the Act 1 "boss," a timed shelter-wide checklist. Ella passes with flying colors (Plinth, through gritted teeth: "...Adequate."). Uncle Dusty shuffles up in a poncho: *"The flood is coming."* Everyone laughs. It's 104°F with zero clouds. **Final shot:** a single raindrop lands on Ella's nose.

## 5. Act 2: Two by Two (Chapters 6 to 10)
**Inciting event: The Forty-Minute Flood.** El Paso gets an entire year's rain in 40 minutes. The arroyos roar, the creosote makes the whole city smell like "rain," and the Ark (built to Uncle Dusty's paranoid specs) **floats for the first time in its life.** It drifts three feet, spins once, and settles back down. When the sun comes out there's a perfect double rainbow over the Franklin Mountains... and **a line of animals two by two stretching to the horizon,** each holding a tiny paper ticket in its mouth.
- **Why?** The narrator explains: "Animals have an ancient instinct. Big boat plus rain equals *board the boat*. It's in the contract." The specific sources come out over the act: a bankrupt New Mexico roadside zoo-and-fireworks outlet ("Ol' Rusty's Critters & Kaboom"), an exotic-pet smuggler's U-Haul that got stuck on I-10 (the smuggler was arrested; the narrator notes "nobody needs a tiger in a U-Haul"), a few that came from "nowhere, honestly," and the dinosaurs and woolly mammoth, which floated over from Dr. Splice's dome.
- **Uncle Dusty, crying in the rain:** *"THIRTY-ONE YEARS. I TOLD Y'ALL. I TOLD EVERYBODY."*

6. **"The Forty-Minute Flood"**: Set piece and intake rush: process the line of exotics at the gangplank (Quick Calls at double speed).
7. **"Some Assembly Required"**: Building habitats: a swamp pool for an alligator (in the desert), a Big Cat Deck, a Reptile Sauna. Lady Chompington the alligator claims her great-great-grandmother lived in San Jacinto Plaza. *Teaches: the habitat upgrade branch and special care.*
8. **"Who Ordered the Dinosaur?"**: A baby T-rex named Tiny arrives in a shipping crate stamped "PROPERTY OF BLACK SANDS LAB." Dr. Splice video-calls to demand his "specimens" back. Plinth, to everyone's shock, sides with Ella: Splice's exotic-animal permit expired in 1997. *"Rules are rules, Doctor."* (He looks surprised at himself.)
9. **"Niche Adopters"**: The first unusual adoptions, from a retired circus clown, the (fictional) Sun City Tech biology lab, a funk-mariachi band and a rancher who "already has a lot of weird stuff." Donations start and the upgrade tree opens wide.
10. **"The Rhino Incident"**: Rhonda the nearsighted rhino charges the KFUN Action News van **live on air** (she thought it was her ex). Trent Dazzleton keeps reporting from the roof of the van. The clip goes national. **Act-out:** that night the flood's leftover watering hole at the bottom of the Ark (everyone calls it "the Puddle") begins to **glow teal.** Up on the ridge, Splice watches through binoculars and whispers, *"Yes..."*

## 6. Act 3: Love Is in the Air (and the Water) (Chapters 11 to 15)
**Inciting event: Romance Hour.** At sunset, the dusty speakers from Uncle Dusty's long-dead "Tunnel of Love" boat ride crackle on by themselves and play a slow funk jam. Fireflies (in El Paso?) appear. Two animals wander to the glowing Puddle, look at each other, and **FWOOMP**: a heart-shaped cloud of glitter covers the screen and a saxophone hits a high note. Cut to sunrise. Gerald the Stork is at the gangplank with a crate: "Sign here." Inside is the first mashup, a **Chihuahuasaurus Rex**.
- **Ella:** "...Doc?" · **Doc Lupe** (long sip of coffee): "Forty-six years I've been a vet. I'm retiring." (beat) "Tomorrow. Today I wanna see what's in the next box."

11. **"Something in the Water"**: The first mashups appear. *Teaches: Romance Hour, the Puddle, mashup crates.*
12. **"Name That Mashup"**: Mashups pile up. The player names, registers and photographs them, and unlocks the **Mash-o-Scope** to preview what any pair would make. A town-hall meeting erupts (see Scene 4).
13. **"Plinth Takes a Sip"**: Plinth arrives to shut everything down. He mistakes a Puddle sample for "complimentary artisanal water," drinks it, and grows a goat beard and a fluffy tail for 24 hours. After a heart-to-heart with Ella on the gangplank ("My mother said pets were unsanitary...") he becomes an ally. *He keeps the beard.*
14. **"The Splice Is Right"**: Ella and Sarge follow the Puddle's pipe over the ridge to Splice's dome. **Twist 1:** Splice isn't a villain, just a very lonely man trying to engineer "a friend who'd never leave." **Twist 2:** his research is bankrolled by **Chase Bottomline of Furever Inc.**, who's been secretly pumping extra Splice-Ade into the Puddle to grow mashups he can patent and clone for his stores. Bottomline rolls up in a gold golf cart and offers $50 million for the whole Ark. Ella: *"They're not products. They're residents."* He smiles: "Everything has a price, sweetheart." She: "Then you can't afford it."
15. **"Overcapacity"**: The Ark is bursting. Local families can't exactly take home a 6-ton slow-motion elephant-sloth. Dakota cuts a "Mashup Monday" video that gets 400 million views overnight. Pop megastar Glitterina comments "OMG I NEED HIM 😭😭😭." **Act-out:** dawn, and the sound of forty helicopters coming over the Franklin Mountains.

## 7. Act 4: Lifestyles of the Rich & Furry (Chapters 16 to 19)
**Inciting event: Helicopter Parents.** The rich and famous land in the desert, kick up a haboob, and line up on a red carpet that Dakota rolled out in 30 seconds. Everyone wants a mashup. Ella sets the rules: **"This is not an auction. The animal picks the home. (The donation box is to your left.)"**

16. **"Helicopter Parents"**: The celebrity Screening Interview mechanic: read the adopter's wants, watch for red flags, match them to the right mashup. Bidding wars break out anyway.
17. **"Red Flags & Red Carpets"**: Bad adopters get exposed. A perfume mogul wants a hippo "as a handbag." A crypto rapper tries to pay in "digital vibes." A rocket billionaire wants to send a rhino to Mars "for science." Ella's rejections become a viral segment, *"Denied by Ella."* Every rejection lowers that adopter's ego and raises the Ark's reputation.
18. **"Happy Tails"**: Postcards start coming back from happy homes (a payoff gag reel), and one mashup comes back with a funny note. Bottomline sends **fake celebrities** (his interns in bad disguises) to steal mashups; the player spots the fakes by tells like a slipping mustache, a Furever Inc. lanyard, or not knowing their own hit song.
19. **"The Billion-Dollar Bark Gala"**: The Ark throws a black-tie fundraiser on deck at sunset under the Star on the Mountain. Ella wears a sparkly gown with her combat boots ("Mission-ready AND runway-ready"). Mid-gala, Bottomline backs a tanker truck up to the Puddle to steal it.

## 8. Finale: The Re-Floodening (Chapter 20)
- The tanker gets stuck, its pump runs backwards, and the Puddle **erupts into a teal geyser** that triggers a freak mini-monsoon. **The Ark floats again**, this time with 200 gala guests on board, and starts drifting toward the arroyo.
- **Set-piece rescue (the game's "boss"):** each adopted mashup's owner calls in their animal and the player triggers mashup powers: the Octeagle airlifts guests, Hot Dogconda becomes a 30-foot rescue rope, Tigeroo bounces people to safety, and the Chihuahuephant shivers so hard the Ark shakes free of a cholla patch. Ella runs it all from the bow like the operation it is.
- Bottomline falls into the geyser and comes out with a striped raccoon tail and a little bandit mask: he's now a literal trash panda ("This is a lawsuit!"). Plinth writes him a ticket for "unlicensed mutation."
- The Ark rides the floodwater and comes to rest **on the slope of the Franklin Mountains, right beside the Star on the Mountain**, where it becomes the city's new landmark: "visible from Las Cruces."
- The Puddle drains into the earth with a big burp. Out of the last bubble climbs **El Grandote**, a house-sized fluffy mashup of *every* animal that has Sarge's face. It just wants a hug. **The City of El Paso adopts it as the official mascot** (parade, key to the city, it high-fives the Star).
- **Resolutions:** Splice becomes the Ark's staff geneticist (reformed; he's finally got friends, and he's "reverse-engineering Churro's hair-tie problem"). Plinth adopts the Slorilla ("We have a lot in common. We both hate Mondays."). Doc Lupe finally retires... and comes back the next morning.
- **The ending:** every animal has a home except one. Ella looks down at Sarge, who has been at her side since Chapter 1. All those years volunteering, never allowed a pet of her own, and now it's her boat and her rules. *"Guess you're stuck with me, partner."* She signs her own adoption form. The Star on the Mountain flickers into a **paw print** for one night. Credits roll over a funk number and a **Happy Tails** montage made of the player's actual adoptions.
- **Post-credits sting:** Gerald the Stork at dawn: "Delivery for the Ark." The crate is addressed from Las Cruces, and it's wiggling. **Sanctuary Mode unlocked** (endless play, all habitats, random mashups forever).

## 9. Villain and rival arcs
| Who | Act 1 | Act 2 | Act 3 | Act 4 | Finale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inspector Plinth** (obstacle → ally) | Inspection boss, wants her to fail | Surprise: sides with her on a technicality | Drinks the Puddle, goat beard, heart-to-heart, becomes an ally | Runs the red-carpet clipboard | Tickets Bottomline, adopts the Slorilla |
| **Dr. Splice** (mad science → friend) | Splice-Ade billboards in the background | Wants his dinosaurs back | Revealed as lonely, not evil | Feeds Ella intel on Bottomline | Becomes staff geneticist |
| **Chase Bottomline** (true villain) | Furever Inc. store opens across the highway ("Why adopt when you can *upgrade?*") | Tries to buy the exotics cheap | Revealed as the money behind the Puddle, offers $50M | Sends fake celebrities, plans the heist | Trash-panda'd and ticketed |

## 10. Twists (in order)
1. **Uncle Dusty was right** (end of Act 1 into Act 2).
2. **The dinosaurs are real** and came from a lab next door (Act 2).
3. **The watering hole is doing it** (Act 3 inciting event). Not the animals' fault, just *very* romantic water.
4. **Plinth is a softie** (Act 3).
5. **Splice isn't the villain; Bottomline is** (Act 3 midpoint).
6. **The fake celebrities** (Act 4).
7. **The Ark ends up on the mountain**, and **El Grandote has Sarge's face** (Finale).
8. **The last adoption is Ella's own** (ending).

## 11. Sample scenes (dialogue and narration)

### Scene 1: Cold open, "A Dollar and a Dream" (Chapter 1)
> *EXT. EL PASO COUNTY SURPLUS AUCTION, A PARKING LOT. 104°F. A tumbleweed rolls past. One folding chair is occupied, by ELLA.*
>
> **NARRATOR:** El Paso, Texas. Three hundred days of sunshine a year. Nine inches of rain. And, for thirty-one years, one ark.
>
> **AUCTIONEER** (dripping sweat): Lot 44. One ark. Wooden. Three hundred feet. Previous owner, quote, "knew the flood was coming." Do I hear one dollar?
>
> *A tumbleweed blows into the empty chair next to Ella.*
>
> **AUCTIONEER:** I have a bid from the tumbleweed—
>
> **ELLA:** One dollar.
>
> **AUCTIONEER:** SOLD to the lady with the hair.
>
> *CUT TO: Ella standing in front of the enormous, sun-bleached Ark. Paint peeling. A faded sign: "UNCLE DUSTY'S FLOOD-PROOF FAMILY FUN ARK. SPLASH ZONE." A cactus is growing out of a porthole.*
>
> **ELLA:** (hands on hips) Okay. Needs a little work.
>
> *A plank falls off. From under the hull, a grey-muzzled German shepherd with dog tags walks out, plants himself between Ella and the Ark, and stares her down.*
>
> **ELLA:** Hey, buddy. You come with the boat?
>
> *SARGE sniffs her boots. Considers. Sits.*
>
> **NARRATOR:** He did come with the boat. A retired military working dog, he had been guarding this ship for six years. Nobody had assigned him. He was not consulted on the sale.
>
> **ELLA:** Well, Sarge, I'm opening an animal shelter. You can be head of security. You're also the only employee. And no, I've never had a dog of my own. Don't make it weird.
>
> *Sarge wags exactly once. TITLE CARD: **ELLA'S ARK.** Funk horns hit.*

>
> **TRENT:** This is Trent Dazzleton, live from Northeast El Paso, where it is... *raining.* Experts are calling it "a lot." Residents are advised to stay indoors, avoid the arroyos, and to stop going outside to smell the creosote. (sniffs) It does smell amazing.
>
> *CUT TO: The Ark, creaking. A loud WOOOONK. It lifts three feet off the sand. Ella stands at the bow with Sarge braced at her side, curls plastered flat.*

>
> **UNCLE DUSTY** (on the deck in a poncho, arms wide, weeping into the sky): THIRTY-ONE YEARS! THEY LAUGHED AT ME AT THE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE! THEY LAUGHED AT ME AT CHURCH BINGO! WHO'S LAUGHING NOW, LINDA?!
>
> *The Ark spins in a slow, majestic circle and sets back down with a THUNK. The rain stops. Instantly. Sun. Double rainbow over the Franklin Mountains.*

>

>
