# Ella's Ark: story & game summary (approved, now built)

> **Approved and built.** Your answers are applied throughout (see the end of this file). Play it at https://antonolson47-ctrl.github.io/ellas-ark/
> Full detail: `STORY.md` · `STORYBOARD.md` · `ANIMALS.md` · `GAME_PLAN.md` · mockups `mockup_keyart_portrait.png`, `mockup_gameplay_landscape.png`, `mockup_mashup_cards.png`

- **The pitch:** Ella buys a giant wooden ark that a local eccentric left in the El Paso desert 31 years ago (she pays $1 at a county auction, and her only competition is a tumbleweed) and turns it into an animal shelter. Things get weirder from there.
- **Act 1, Grand Opening:** dogs and cats arrive fast. You feed, wash, vet, play with and clean up after them, post adoption ads with a teen volunteer, and match pets to the right families. Quick swipe decisions keep it moving ("Kevin is stuck in a cabinet. Again."). The act ends with a county inspection and a strange warning that "the flood is coming."
- **Act 2, Two by Two (the inciting event):** El Paso gets a year of rain in 40 minutes, the Ark actually floats, and when the sun comes out there's a line of animals two by two all the way to the mountains: alligators, lions, a rhino, penguins, elephants, and dinosaurs that escaped from a mad scientist's lab over the ridge.
- **Act 3, Love Is in the Air (and the Water):** the flood leaves a glowing "Puddle." At sunset an old Tunnel-of-Love speaker plays a slow jam, two animals wander over, a heart-shaped glitter cloud covers the screen, and at dawn a grumpy gig-economy stork delivers a crate. Out comes a Chihuahuasaurus Rex. It's cartoon-funny and **completely off-screen.**
- **Act 4, Lifestyles of the Rich & Furry:** a viral video brings helicopters full of parody billionaires and celebrities (all made-up names and looks). Ella screens them like a boss, catches the red flags ("Mars," "as a handbag") and stamps **DENIED BY ELLA** on the bad ones. Their donations run into the millions.
- **Villains and twists:** a joyless county inspector who turns into an ally (after he drinks the Puddle and grows a goat beard), a lonely mad scientist who isn't really evil, and the true villain, Chase Bottomline of *Furever Inc.*, who's secretly juicing the Puddle to patent mashups. **Finale:** his heist sets off a second flood, the mashups use their weird powers to save everyone, the Ark ends up on the mountain next to the Star, and Ella's last adoption is her own: Sarge, the retired military working dog who's been at her side since Chapter 1 (she's volunteered at shelters for years but never been allowed a pet of her own).
- **Money and upgrades:** every unusual adoption brings a donation, from $50 dog adoptions up to $8 million rocket-billionaire gifts. You spend it on six upgrade branches: Habitats (a swamp pool in the desert, a Jurassic paddock, eventually a second ark), Care Tech (an animal car wash, poop-powered A/C), Outreach (I-10 billboard, radio jingle, a helipad for the celebs), Staff (Doc Lupe, the Abuela Knitting Brigade, Ella's crew), the Puddle Lab (pick which animals mash, preview the result) and Ark Glam (looks).
- **Mashups never get old:** 25 hand-made legendary mashups, plus a generator that builds about 1,400 more from animal "parts." It always picks the funniest version (a tiny head on a huge body, wings on a rhino) and writes a name and a joke for each one.
- **Ella is the hero:** she's the competent, funny one in every scene, her Army grit shows up as light style only ("Adapt. Overcome. Adopt."), never a base, rank or job, and her blond curls bounce and boing. Jokes are never at her expense or the Army's, and they're never at El Paso's (the local details are accurate, and there are no saguaros).
- **Humor:** for adults, South Park level: raunchy and envelope-pushing (crude jokes, innuendo, profanity, gross-out). Mashups stay cartoonish and off-screen, with no explicit sex or nudity, nothing hateful, and no real people, brands or politics. A settings toggle switches between **Raunchy** (default) and **Cleaner**.
- **Music:** 10 original funk tracks in a James Brown-*style* sound: horn stabs, chicken-scratch guitar, slap bass, organ, "Hah!" shouts. All melodies and lyrics are new, plus a sound effect for every action.
- **Same tech as your other games:** one HTML file on GitHub Pages that works in portrait and landscape on phones, with no ads and no purchases. Roughly 8 to 10 hours of story, then an endless Sanctuary Mode.

## The best 8 regular & unusual animals
1. **Chancla** (chihuahua): 4 lbs and thinks she's a Rottweiler. Barks at the wind.
2. **Kevin** (orange cat): one brain cell, shared with no one. Gets stuck in cabinets.
3. **Boots** (tuxedo cat): escape artist. Ella calls him "AWOL."
4. **Sarge** (retired military working dog): dignified and wise, Ella's sidekick all game, and her own adoption in the ending.
5. **Lady Chompington** (alligator): says her great-great-grandma lived in San Jacinto Plaza. Wants to be a lap dog, at 11 feet long.
6. **Rhonda** (rhino): nearsighted, charges anything that looks like her ex (a beige sedan), wears tiny glasses.
7. **Tiny** (baby T-rex): arms too short to clap; sad at birthday parties.
8. **Maracas** (rattlesnake): rattles in perfect 16th notes and drums in the Ark's funk band.

## The best 10 mashups
1. **Chihuahuasaurus Rex** (chihuahua × T-rex): its roar is a squeaky toy. Adopted by a pop megastar; it eats her purse.
2. **Hot Dogconda** (dachshund × python): 30 feet long; takes four minutes to finish wagging. Goes to a limo mogul.
3. **Bald Rhin-Eagle** (rhino × eagle): can't fly, glides downhill screaming patriotically. A rocket billionaire adopts it, on the condition that it stays on Earth.
4. **Pengator** (penguin × alligator): needs a swamp AND air conditioning. A yacht billionaire calls it "finally, a butler that bites."
5. **Tigeroo** (tiger × kangaroo): bounces 30 feet with stolen car keys in its pouch. Goes to a basketball star.
6. **Chihuahuephant** (chihuahua × elephant): its shivering shows up on the Richter scale. Goes to a Texas oil tycoon.
7. **Lioctopus** (lion × octopus): a mane of tentacles; holds eight steaks and eight grudges. Goes to a screaming TV chef.
8. **Rattleriever** (golden retriever × rattlesnake): rattles when happy, which is always. Joins a funk-mariachi band.
9. **Woolly Meowmoth** (Persian cat × woolly mammoth): six tons of judgmental floof that sheds 300 lbs a day.
10. **Slorilla** (gorilla × sloth): the strongest animal on Earth, refuses to get up. The inspector adopts it: "We both hate Mondays."
