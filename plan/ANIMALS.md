## 4. How random mashups are generated (so every combo is funny)
The 25 legends above are hand-made. Every other pair (38 species gives 703 pairs, each with 2 "which parent gives the face" versions, so **~1,400 combos**) comes from a part-based generator built for comedy.

### 4.1 Every species is a "parts kit"
Each species is defined in data as a rig of swappable parts on standard sockets:
- **Face** (head shape, eyes, ears, mouth/beak/snout, voice), **Body** (body plan: quadruped / biped / serpent / bird / aquatic / multi-leg), **Legs set**, **Tail**, **Skin** (fur / scales / feathers / smooth / wool / bristle), **Pattern** (stripes, spots, tuxedo, beaded, solid), and **Signature parts** (trunk, horn, mane, wings, rattle, frill, tentacles, pouch, antlers, tusks, flippers, crest).
- Stats: **size class** (1 = Chancla … 6 = Peanut/Willie), **cuteness** (1 to 5), **scariness** (1 to 5), **habitat** (desert, swamp, ice, jungle, sky, saltwater), **diet**, 3 **personality traits**, 1 **quirk** and a **voice** (bark, squeak, roar, hiss, honk, screech, rattle, tenor).

### 4.2 Assembly rules ("readable first, ridiculous second")
1. **One parent gives the FACE, the other gives the BODY PLAN.** You can always tell who the parents are from the face plus one signature part.
2. **Each parent must contribute at least one signature part** (the trunk, the horn, the mane, the wings...). If a species has none (dogs, cats), its signature part is its *personality*, shown as a pose/expression and an accessory (Kevin's blank stare, Tamale's blanket).
3. **Maximum five visible "features"** so the silhouette stays readable at phone size. A silhouette test checks the thumbnail outline is distinct from both parents.
4. **Size is decided by the body donor; the face is scaled up or down** (a chihuahua head on an elephant body, or a T-rex body at 4 lbs). Big mismatches are encouraged.
5. Colors: the body donor's base color, the face donor's accent color, and a **Puddle tint** (a 10% teal glow on eyes or markings) so every mashup reads as "Puddle-made."

### 4.3 The "Funny Score" (the generator picks the funniest version)
For each pair, the generator builds 6 candidate assemblies (seeded randomness, so the same pair always gives the same result) and keeps the one with the highest score:
| Factor | Points | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Size contrast** (face vs body size class) | +3 per class of difference | Chihuahua face on elephant body = +15 |
| **Cute/scary flip** (cute face on a scary body, or vice versa) | +6 | Kitten face on an alligator |
| **Physics fail** (wings on a size 5+, flippers on a desert animal, tiny legs on a long body) | +5 each | Rhino with eagle wings: "Can't fly. Has never been told." |
| **Habitat clash** (ice + desert, swamp + sky) | +4 | Penguin + Gila monster |
| **Voice mismatch** (big body + squeak, tiny body + roar) | +4 | |
| **Personality contradiction** (lazy + hyper, shy + diva) | +3 | Sloth + roadrunner |
| **Readability** penalty | -10 if the silhouette is too close to either parent or has more than 5 features | |

### 4.4 Names
- Each species has hand-written **name fragments**: prefixes and suffixes plus "pun hooks" (Chihua-/-huahua, Rhin-/-ceros, Ele-/-phant, Pen-/-guin, Gila-, -gator, Purr-, Pew-, Meow-, Hot Dog-, -saurus, -opus, -roo, -rilla, -lope, -cabra...).
- The algorithm tries **prefixA + suffixB** and **prefixB + suffixA** and scores them on pronounceability (no triple consonants, vowel joins smoothed), length (2 to 4 syllables) and a **pun bonus** from a dictionary of real words and phrases ("Hot Dog," "Purr," "Bald"). The best one wins.
- An **override table** holds about 80 hand-picked names for the best pairs (all 25 legends, plus about 55 rares).
- Every mashup also gets a **title** from its quirk: "Gilbert Jr., the Perpetually Confused," "Lady Floofington, Destroyer of Lint Rollers."
- The player can rename any mashup (preset name parts, or typed text that's length-limited and word-filtered).

### 4.5 Personality and gag lines
- **Personality** = one trait from each parent + one **Puddle Quirk** drawn from a table of about 50 (it thinks it's a houseplant, it only communicates in sneezes, it's afraid of its own tail, it does taxes, it loves Mondays, it believes it's a famous DJ, it's "on a cleanse," it collects bottle caps, it insists on being called "Doctor"...).
- **Gag line** = picked from about 90 templates keyed to whatever scored highest in its Funny Score, so the joke always points at the funniest thing about *this* animal:
  - Size contrast: *"Weighs {weight}. Still thinks it's a lap {face species}."*
  - Physics fail: *"Has {part}. Has never once used them correctly."*
  - Habitat clash: *"Needs a {habitatA} and a {habitatB}. Currently lives in your bathtub."*
  - Voice mismatch: *"{Size} feet of muscle. Sounds like a {voice}."*
  - Contradiction: *"Wants to {trait A verb}. Also wants to {trait B verb}. Mostly just yells."*
  - Quirk: *"Insists on being called Doctor. Has not been to medical school."*
- **Bios** for the Mashdex use 3-sentence Mad Libs filled from the data, with a hand-polish pass on all the rares.

### 4.6 Care and adoption tags from parts
- **Diet** = both parents' diets ("eats kibble AND hay; we don't ask"). **Habitat** = the more demanding of the two. **Signature care** = one mini-task from each parent, combined (brush the mane + polish the shell).
- Parts generate **adoption tags**: wings → *Flyer*, horn → *Tough*, fluffy → *Cuddly*, size 5+ → *Needs Acreage*, aquatic → *Pool*, loud voice → *Not for Apartments*, Puddle Quirk → a fun extra tag like *Party Animal* or *Introvert*. Celebrity adopters want specific tags (ANIMALS §5, GAME_PLAN §5).

### 4.7 Rarity and how the player steers it
| Rarity | Source | Share | Visuals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | Generator, any pair | ~70% | Generated rig, standard idle animation |
| **Rare** | Override-table pairs | ~25% | Hand-tuned parts, a unique idle animation, a special name |
| **Legendary** | The 25 above | ~5% (guaranteed through story beats) | Custom art, a unique entrance animation, a Happy Tails ending |
| **Mythic** | "Blue Moon Romance Hour" (3 parents), finale | Rare event | El Grandote and a few triple-mashups in Sanctuary Mode |

The **Love Bench** and **Mash-o-Scope** upgrades (GAME_PLAN §6) let the player choose which two residents go to the Puddle and preview a blurry silhouette of the result, so the player can chase the whole Mashdex on purpose.
