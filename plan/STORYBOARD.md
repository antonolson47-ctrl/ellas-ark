# Ella's Ark: Storyboard (14 panels, opening to finale)

> Each panel lists what's on screen, what the player does and the joke. **Phone frame:** portrait is the primary layout, and every panel also has a landscape version (GAME_PLAN §10).
> Concept art: `mockup_keyart_portrait.png` (Panel 1 look), `mockup_gameplay_landscape.png` (Panel 4/7 look), `mockup_mashup_cards.png` (Panel 9/11 look).

---

### Panel 1: Title screen, "All Creatures Welcome"
- **On screen:** A sunset over the Franklin Mountains (orange to magenta to violet), with the **Star on the Mountain** glowing on the ridge. The giant wooden Ark sits on the desert flats among prickly pear, ocotillo and yucca. Tumbleweeds roll through. Ella stands in front, curls bouncing in the wind, with Biscuit at her boots. Logo: **ELLA'S ARK**, carved wood lettering with a paw-print "A."
- **Player does:** Taps "PLAY." Ella's curls *boing.*
- **Joke:** A small sign on the hull reads "ALL CREATURES WELCOME (SERIOUSLY. ALL.)." A tourist walks past pointing at an ocotillo: "Look, a saguaro!" The narrator, offscreen: "No."
- **Audio:** *"Ark Funk (Ella's Theme)"*: horn-section hit, then the groove drops on the one.

### Panel 2: Cold open, the $1 auction
- **On screen:** A sun-baked county parking lot, one folding chair, an auctioneer melting. Then a reveal: the peeling Ark with a cactus growing out of a porthole.
- **Player does:** Taps the bid paddle (the whole tutorial is one button). "SOLD." Then a **Quick Call** swipe card: *"There's a dog guarding the boat." ← Salute him / Hire him as head of security →*. (Both choices lead to Biscuit joining, but the narrator comments differently.)
- **Joke:** The other bidder is a tumbleweed. Sarge (a retired military working dog guarding his self-assigned post) "came with the boat" and was "not consulted on the sale."

### Panel 3: First intake, "Here come the dogs"
- **On screen:** A beat-up rescue van backs up to the gangplank. Out tumble Chancla, Kevin and Lowrider. The kennel deck has 4 kennels, each with a needs ring around the animal (🍖🛁🩺🎾🧹).
- **Player does:** Taps an animal to open the **care wheel**, drags a food bowl, scrubs to wash (bubbles, then a shake-dry that splashes Ella) and swipes poop into a bucket.
- **Joke:** Chancla barks at a tumbleweed until it rolls away, then turns to the player: *"You're welcome."* Kevin walks into a wall. A pop-up reads **"Kevin has learned nothing."**

### Panel 4: The Care Rush (the core moment-to-moment)
- **On screen:** Six kennels, meters dropping, a clock running toward 4:00 PM Open House. Quick Call cards slide in from the side: *"Churro ate a hair tie." ← Vet now / Wait and see →*. Mid-rush, a spring **dust storm** rolls in and every animal turns paper-bag beige.
- **Player does:** Prioritizes: taps, drags and swipes. Combos (feed → wash → play the same animal in a row) build a **"Hooah Streak."**
- **Joke:** After the haboob, Ella's curls have doubled in size. Doc Lupe: "Mija, you look like a dandelion." Ella: "Tactical volume."

### Panel 5: The Ad Builder, "Adopt-a-Gram"
- **On screen:** Dakota's phone fills the screen. Choose a **photo pose** (3 snapshots taken earlier during care), **2 tag stickers** ("Couch Potato," "Good With Kids," "Escape Artist") and a **channel** (Adopt-a-Gram, a flyer at the farmers market, a billboard on I-10, later local TV and radio).
- **Player does:** Drags stickers onto the photo and taps POST. Likes tick up.
- **Joke:** The first post gets 11 likes. Dakota: *"We're basically famous."* One comment, from "LindaFromChurchBingo": "is this the flood guy's boat."

### Panel 6: Open House, adoption matching
- **On screen:** Adopters walk up the gangplank with thought-bubble **wish cards** (icons for home size, kids, energy level). Animals are lined up on the left.
- **Player does:** Drags an animal onto an adopter. A **Match Meter** fills (hearts). Taps one **Interview question** to reveal a hidden red or green flag. Confirm, and a coin shower plays with an adoption certificate stamp.
- **Joke:** Brody the gym bro wants a dog "for the 'gram." The red flag reveals itself: he named his last dog "Content." Denied. The Holloways adopt Sir Barksalot, who takes four minutes to walk to their car.

### Panel 7: Inspection Day (Act 1 boss)
- **On screen:** Inspector Plinth's clipboard frames the screen as a giant checklist HUD. He walks deck to deck with a 90-second timer.
- **Player does:** Races ahead of him fixing things (scrub a kennel, refill water, catch Boots ("AWOL") on the roof).
- **Joke:** Plinth finds nothing wrong, which visibly upsets him: *"...Adequate."* Act out: Uncle Dusty in a poncho says "The flood is coming." Sun, 104°F, zero clouds. **A raindrop lands on Ella's nose.** Smash cut to black. Thunder.

### Panel 8: The Forty-Minute Flood (Act 2 inciting event)
- **On screen:** Rain sheets down and the arroyos rush. The Ark creaks, lifts three feet and spins once. Uncle Dusty weeps with joy on deck. The sun cuts through and a double rainbow arcs over the Franklin Mountains. **The camera pans along a line of animals, two by two, to the horizon.**
- **Player does:** Holds to "steady the Ark" (a short tilt mini-game), then a rapid gangplank intake sorting cards into habitats (swipe left = big, right = bitey, up = "see me directly").
- **Joke:** A triceratops stands alone holding a sign: "+1?" Dusty: *"WHO'S LAUGHING NOW, LINDA?!"*

### Panel 9: Habitats and exotic care
- **On screen:** A cutaway of the Ark like a dollhouse, with new decks bolted on: the **Swamp Pool** (a pool on the top deck, in the desert), the **Big Cat Deck**, the **Reptile Sauna** and the **Jurassic Paddock** (a fenced sandlot off the stern).
- **Player does:** Exotic care mini-tasks: tongs-feed the gator (timing), blow-dry King Darryl's mane (swirl), mud-paint Rhonda (drag), hold-to-heat Gilbert's rock (gauge), hug-release from Noodle (tap mash).
- **Joke:** Peanut the elephant gives herself a trunk shower and always sprays Ella. Ella, soaked: "...Refreshing. Roger that." Tiny can't reach the "clap" button on the fanfare.

### Panel 10: The Rhino Incident (Act 2 climax)
- **On screen:** A live KFUN Action News 6 broadcast. Trent Dazzleton is mid-report. Rhonda charges the beige news van. Trent keeps reporting **from the van's roof.** A chyron reads "BREAKING: RHINO."
- **Player does:** A chase mini-game: drag a giant carrot on a fishing line to steer Rhonda back home.
- **Joke:** The chyron updates: "STILL RHINO." Then: "RHINO SAYS SORRY." The clip goes viral (the Adopt-a-Gram counter jumps from 11 to 11 million). **Act out:** night, and the Puddle glows teal. Splice's binoculars glint on the ridge.

### Panel 11: Romance Hour and Gerald's delivery (Act 3 inciting event)
- **On screen:** Sunset. The old "Tunnel of Love" speakers crackle on with a slow funk jam (*"Puddle Love"*). Two animals walk to the glowing Puddle, look at each other... **FWOOMP**: a heart-shaped glitter cloud with a saxophone high note covers the scene. Cut to dawn. Gerald the Stork, in a StorkDash vest, has a wiggling crate.
- **Player does:** Signs for the delivery (swipe a signature), then taps the crate repeatedly to pry it open → **REVEAL CARD** (a trading-card flip with the name, parents, rarity, a funny bio and the first Mashdex entry).
- **Joke:** It's a Chihuahuasaurus Rex. Its roar is a squeaky toy. Gerald: *"Rate me five stars. I got four more crates and a bad hip. ...Five. One's humming."*

### Panel 12: The Town Hall and the Splice twist (Act 3 middle)
- **On screen:** Split panel. **Left:** a chaotic town hall, with folding chairs, a banner and Plinth at the podium (now with a goat beard from his Puddle sip). **Right:** Ella and Sarge at Splice's mirrored dome, where Bottomline arrives in a gold golf cart.
- **Player does:** Town hall: pick Ella's reply cards to calm the room (each correct pick adds "Public Trust"). Dome: a **dialogue showdown** with Bottomline. Pick 3 answers; every one is a burn, and the player chooses which.
- **Joke:** "Who's gonna buy me a 30-foot leash?!" Then Bottomline: "Everything has a price, sweetheart." Ella: "Then you can't afford it." Sarge lifts a leg and pees on the golf cart. (Off-screen, implied by a puddle and a sound effect. PG.)

### Panel 13: Red Carpet Screening (Act 4)
- **On screen:** Helicopters land in a haboob of their own making. A red carpet rolls up the gangplank. Celebrity adopters queue with entourages. The **Screening Interview** UI shows the adopter's portrait (left), the mashup (right), a Match Meter, and red/green flag slots that unlock as you ask questions. A big **DONATION** thermometer sits on the side.
- **Player does:** Asks 2 of 4 interview questions, reads the answers, then Approves or **Denies with a one-liner** (choose from 3 roasts). Bidding wars pop up as a fast tap-off ("Not an auction!"), but the donation pot still grows.
- **Joke:** Dex Orbitz wants to take the Rhin-Eagle to Mars. Ella: "You are the people for that." Madame Odeur asks for the hippo "as a handbag." **DENIED BY ELLA** stamps across the screen and becomes a viral segment.

### Panel 14: The Re-Floodening and the finale
- **On screen:** The night gala on deck. Bottomline's tanker rams the Puddle, a teal geyser erupts and the Ark floats again with 200 guests aboard. The rescue sequence calls in mashup powers (Octeagle airlift, Hot Dogconda rope bridge, Tigeroo bounce pad, Chihuahuephant shiver-quake). The Ark comes to rest beside the Star on the Mountain. El Grandote climbs out of the last bubble.
- **Player does:** A "command the operation" boss: tap each mashup's power button when its icon lights to save guests (big, generous timing windows), all set to the finale funk track. Then the ending: Ella and Biscuit alone on the bow and one last adoption form. **The player signs it.**
- **Joke:** Bottomline comes out of the geyser with a raccoon tail and mask, a literal trash panda, and Plinth tickets him for "unlicensed mutation." El Grandote has Sarge's face. The Star on the Mountain flickers into a **paw print.** Post-credits: Gerald, with a crate "from Las Cruces." It's humming.
- **Audio:** *"Star on the Mountain (Finale Funk)"*: full horns, a key change, a big ending stab, then a Happy Tails postcard montage of the player's real adoptions.
